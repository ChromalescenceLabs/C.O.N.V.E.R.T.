# C.A.N.I.N.E.
Contains the files for the game C.A.N.I.N.E., created for the 2026 GMTK Game Jam, and submitted to Hack Club's Jame Gam. Play it here: https://chromalescence-labs.itch.io/project-canine.

## Summary
### I. Lore
For the past decade, crime rates have skyrocketed, leaving the once prosperous city in ruins. As crime continued to rise, prisons reached a breaking point. Those built to hold hundreds had to accommodate thousands. Faced with no other choice, the government introduced the Correctional Assignment for Necessary Institutionalized National Enforcement or C.A.N.I.N.E. 

The program proposed an alternative to imprisonment. Instead of serving their life behind bars, eligible convicts could dedicate themselves to enforcing the law under strict government supervision. Those who prove themselves through their service could be let out on parole, transforming convicted felons into reformed officers. 

Or at least that’s what they told you.

You were one of the many people who were eligible for the program. You were sent to a correctional facility, stripped off your name and dignity. You lashed out. It wasn't like how they made it out to be to the public. They treated you worse than animals. Ever since they put that damned muzzle on your face when you first retaliated, you vowed to yourself that you'd take down the Handler, the one controlling the entire facility.

## How to Run
1. Go to this link: https://chromalescence-labs.itch.io/project-canine, no need to install anything on your device.
2. Click on the "Run Game" button found on the page.
3. (Hopefully) Enjoy playing the game!
4. Feel free to give us constructive criticism - we're beginners and are always open to respectful advice.
